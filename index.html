Nenjah Bot

Nenjah Bot is the private stream toolkit used by DJ Nenjah to run his own YouTube channel (youtube.com/@DJNenjah). It is a desktop program that runs on DJ Nenjah's own computer. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Uploads and schedules DJ Nenjah's own edited videos and Shorts to his own channel, with titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists he approves.
  • Sets the title and category of his own live streams.
  • Reads his own live chat for chat commands and text-to-speech, and shows his live viewer count on stream.
  • Reads his own channel analytics to decide which clips to make next.

Nenjah Bot uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
